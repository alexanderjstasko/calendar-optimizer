What it does

One small server on your own machine, a calendar app on your phone.

One calendar

Google, Apple, Outlook and Partiful synced every few minutes into one Day, Week and Month view. Read-only: it never writes to your calendars.

A weekly plan

Every Sunday Claude places your tasks and recurring goals around your fixed events, sleep, work hours and a free-time floor you choose.

You decide

Each block is a proposal. Approve a whole series with one tap, move a single session, or reject it. The planner learns from your choices.

Keeps up during the week

A new Notion item or a meeting landing on a planned block produces a suggested change for you to review, never a silent edit.

Events worth going to

A scout reads event listings you choose and offers talks, shows and meetups that fit your free evenings and interests.

Built for your phone

Installs as a home-screen app, works offline from the last sync, and sends a review email when a new plan is ready.

How it works

Notion holds what you want to do. Your calendars hold what is already fixed. The app puts them together.

  1. SyncCalendar events and your Notion tree (Telos, goals, projects, routines, tasks) land in a local SQLite database.
  2. PlanRoutines are placed by code; flexible goals and tasks go to Claude with your week, your hours and your stated preferences.
  3. ReviewProposals appear in the Plan tab and in a preview on the calendar. Approve, move or reject them.
  4. TrackAfter an approved block has passed, the app writes the time spent and the last session back to Notion.

Setup

You need Python 3.11+, Node 20.19+, a Notion integration token, and Claude: an Anthropic API key or a Claude subscription with the claude CLI.

git clone https://github.com/alexanderjstasko/calendar-optimizer.git
cd calendar-optimizer
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r backend/requirements.txt -r backend/requirements-dev.txt
cp .env.example .env      # fill in your tokens and calendar links
(cd frontend && npm install && npm run build)
.venv/bin/python3 -m uvicorn --app-dir backend main:app --port 8000

Open http://localhost:8000/, then set your sleep, work hours and home address in the Hours tab. The README covers every setting, Google Calendar sign-in and the approval emails.

For everyday use, run it on an always-on machine such as a Raspberry Pi 4 and reach it from your phone over Tailscale. The Pi guide sets up the service, updates and a daily backup.

Notion setup

One database holds everything, as a tree with sub-items turned on. Share it with your integration, or Notion's API answers 404.

Your data stays yours

Everything runs on your machine: events, plans and tokens live in a local database and .env. The only outside calls are to your calendar feeds, Notion, Claude and, if you turn them on, your mail server and the event sites you list.
